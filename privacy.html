Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025  ·  Effective: January 1, 2025  ·  Version 1.0

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information. We are committed to being transparent and never selling your personal data.

1. Who We Are

HelpHub AI ("we," "us," "our") is a community-powered support network platform. This Privacy Policy applies to the HelpHub AI website and all associated services. For privacy inquiries, contact us at privacy@helphubai.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, as well as information generated through your use of the Platform.

Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, password (stored as a secure hash), and your chosen role (help-seeker, helper, or both).

Google Sign-In Data: If you authenticate using Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL, and Google User ID from Google's OAuth service. We do not receive your Google password.

Profile Data: Optional information you choose to add such as your location, skills, interests, and bio.

Usage Data: Help requests you post, responses you provide, messages sent, Trust Score activity, badges earned, and leaderboard contributions.

Technical Data: Browser type, device type, IP address, pages visited, and session duration. This data is used for analytics and security purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate your identity
  • Operate core platform features: help requests, messaging, leaderboards, notifications
  • Power AI matching and categorization features
  • Calculate and display Trust Scores and reputation badges
  • Send account-related emails (security alerts, welcome emails)
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the Platform
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for targeted advertising. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage

HelpHub AI currently stores user data in your browser's local storage (localStorage) for session management. Firebase Authentication is used for Google Sign-In, and Firebase Analytics is used for anonymized usage metrics. Your data is stored on Google's Firebase infrastructure, which is subject to Google's security and compliance standards.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit and secure credential handling via Firebase.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

HelpHub AI uses browser localStorage to store your session information, preferences, and application data. This is not the same as traditional cookies, but serves a similar purpose of persisting your state between visits.

Firebase may set cookies for authentication purposes. These are essential cookies necessary for the Platform to function and cannot be disabled without preventing you from signing in.

We use Firebase Analytics with anonymized data (IP anonymization enabled) to understand usage patterns. You may opt out of analytics tracking by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication (Google Sign-In), Analytics, and infrastructure. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — Typography (Plus Jakarta Sans, DM Sans). Google may log font requests. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not share your personal data with these services beyond what is necessary to provide the Platform's features.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete data via your Profile settings.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data from your Profile settings. Note that some data may be retained for legal compliance purposes.
  • Portability: Request an export of your data in a common format.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for analytics purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@helphubai.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

HelpHub AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

Users between 13 and 18 years of age must have parental or guardian consent to use the Platform.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion, we may retain anonymized or aggregated data for analytical purposes. Help request content may remain visible in anonymized form for community value even after account deletion.

10. International Users

HelpHub AI is operated from Pakistan. If you access the Platform from outside Pakistan, your data may be transferred to and processed in Pakistan and on Google's global infrastructure. By using the Platform, you consent to this transfer.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or a prominent notice on the Platform. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Privacy inquiries: privacy@helphubai.com
  • General inquiries: hello@helphubai.com
  • Legal matters: legal@helphubai.com
