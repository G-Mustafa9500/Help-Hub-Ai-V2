HOW IT WORKS

Describe your problem clearly. The AI auto-detects the category and urgency level for you.

2

Community Sees It

Your request goes live on the Explore feed. Anyone with relevant skills can browse and find it.

3

4

GET HELP

How to get help on HelpHub AI

📝 Write a clear title

A specific title like "React useState not re-rendering on object update" gets better responses than "Help with code".

🏷️ Use the right tags

Tags help helpers find your request faster. Add the tools, languages, or skills involved.

⚡ Set urgency honestly

High urgency gets prioritized by helpers. Only mark as High if you genuinely have a deadline.

✅ Mark it solved

Once you get a working solution, mark your request as solved so helpers know their time was well spent.

GIVE HELP

How to help others effectively

🔍 Browse the Explore feed

Filter by category, urgency, or location to find requests that match your skills and availability.

💬 Post a public reply

Click "Open" on any request, write your solution in the reply box, and attach images if needed. All replies are public.

📸 Attach screenshots

Images make solutions much clearer. Attach code screenshots, diagrams, or annotated images directly in your reply.

⭐ Earn Trust Score

Every accepted solution earns you contribution points and increases your Trust Score. Top helpers appear on the Leaderboard.
